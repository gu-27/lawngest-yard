About

Just a guy who loves
a great lawn.

The Lawngest Yard started with a simple idea: what if someone who isn't a professional tackled their lawn head-on and documented everything? The wins, the failures, the experiments — all of it.

Based in the Pacific Northwest, the channel covers everything from overseeding and fertilizing to product reviews and full lawn transformations. The philosophy is simple: you don't need to be an expert to have an amazing lawn. You just need to be willing to put in the work — and try things a few times.

Whether you're battling weeds, dealing with bare patches, or starting completely from scratch, there's something here for you.
